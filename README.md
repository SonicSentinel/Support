# Sonic Sentinel: support

Help, bug reports and feature requests for **Sonic Sentinel**, the acoustic burst watch for macOS and iOS.

- **Report a problem or ask for something:** [open an issue](https://github.com/SonicSentinel/Support/issues/new/choose)
- **Common questions:** the [support page](https://sonicsentinel.github.io/support/) answers the usual ones (microphone permission, why no ultrasound shows, room-noise calibration, notifications, your data)
- **Privacy:** [privacy policy](https://sonicsentinel.github.io/privacy/): the app collects nothing
- **More about the app:** [sonicsentinel.github.io](https://sonicsentinel.github.io/)

This repository holds no source code. It is only the public place to ask for help.

## Before you post

Issues here are **public**, and GitHub, not the app, handles them. Please do not post anything private:

- no recordings or audio of other people, and no addresses or locations you do not want public;
- exported reports and logs are **encrypted by default** and contain detections (time, frequency, level), never audio. If you want to attach one, decrypt it first (Report tab, or `SonicSentinel --decrypt file`) and check it contains nothing you would not post;
- the encryption salt is a secret: never paste it.

## What helps most

The app version (About tab), your macOS or iOS version, the audio input and sample rate (shown in the header as "up to ... kHz"), what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot is often enough. If it is about speed, turn on **Settings > Graphics > Show performance panel** and include a screenshot of it.

## Security or privacy concerns

Please do not use a public issue for these. Get in touch through [danoli3.com](https://danoli3.com).
